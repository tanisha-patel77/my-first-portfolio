About Me

I am a 17-year-old student currently living in Rwanda. Born and brought up in Kenya with roots originating from India, my multicultural background gives me a unique perspective on community, leadership, and problem-solving.

My passion for computer science runs deep—everyone at school knows it's my absolute favorite domain! Beyond writing code, I love diving into hands-on technology: I have configured routers, worked extensively with computer networking, tinkered with hardware like Arduino, and solved complex logic problems.
